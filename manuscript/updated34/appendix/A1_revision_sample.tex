\section{Preferred revision sample}\label{app:events}

The preferred sample is the result of a two-stage screen applied to the 127 revisions detected at the baseline PCSI
threshold. The mechanical stage removes overlapping event windows and events without usable core AUTM outcomes. All 62
revisions surviving that stage were then reviewed at the document-pair level. Thirty-four satisfy C/P comparability and
A/B timing. Table~\ref{tab:events} reports all 34 events. The ``second pass'' label identifies the nine events admitted
after reviewing the 37 mechanically eligible pairs that had not been part of the September-25 event set.
