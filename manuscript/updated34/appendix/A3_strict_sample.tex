\section{Stricter post-support sensitivity}\label{app:strict}

The strict sample applies one additional rule uniformly to all documentary-reviewed events: the treated institution must
have at least two core-outcome panel years in event times 0 through $+5$, guaranteeing at least one observed calendar year
after the revision year. This removes five preferred-sample events and leaves 29 revisions (7 upward, 22 downward).
